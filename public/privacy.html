Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026 | Last Updated: September 24, 2026

Welcome to Attendzy ("we", "us", "our", or "Application"). This Privacy Policy explains how Attendzy collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our web application at https://attendzy.netlify.app and related services.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver student attendance tracking, timetable management, and academic percentage analytics.

Summary: Attendzy does NOT sell, rent, or monetize your data. We do NOT use your information for advertising, tracking across third-party websites, or artificial intelligence training.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to the Application:

  • Google Account Information: When you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, we receive your basic profile information (such as your display name, email address, and profile picture avatar) provided via Firebase Authentication. We use this strictly to identify your session and authenticate your account.
  • Academic & Timetable Data: Information you input or manage within the app, including subject names, course codes, instructor names, timetable schedules, classroom timings, and target attendance percentage goals.
  • Attendance Records: Daily class attendance status marks (Present, Absent, Excused, or Cancelled) and any optional notes you add to specific class sessions.
  • Uploaded Timetable Images: If you upload a photo or screenshot of your timetable for schedule extraction, the image is processed solely for recognizing class schedules and is not stored or shared for external purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following operational purposes:

  • To calculate your overall and subject-wise attendance percentages.
  • To compute safe "bunk" allowances and attendance deficits required to meet your percentage goals.
  • To synchronize your subjects, daily marks, and timetable across your personal devices.
  • To authenticate your user profile and safeguard your private attendance records.

3. Google User Data & Limited Use Policy

Attendzy uses Google Sign-In solely for user authentication. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We only request non-sensitive identity scopes (openid, profile, and email).
  • We do NOT transfer Google user data to third parties, advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.
  • We do NOT use Google user data to serve advertisements or personalized marketing.
  • We do NOT use or transfer Google user data to train generalized AI or machine learning models.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication, utilizing industry-standard transport layer security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Access to your personal database documents is protected by Firebase Security Rules ensuring that only your authenticated account can read or write your records.

5. Local Storage and Cookies

We use browser localStorage solely to remember your offline session preferences (such as your chosen weekend days, target percentage, and active view) so the application functions properly across browser refreshes. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

You maintain complete ownership of your academic records. You can delete your attendance data at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete individual subjects, reset daily schedules, or click Settings > Clear All Data to permanently erase all attendance marks, timetable schedules, and subjects from the database.
  • Account Deletion Request: You can request full account removal and database deletion by contacting us at burnster1826@gmail.com. Upon receipt, all account data will be permanently wiped.

7. Children's Privacy

Attendzy is an educational utility designed for high school and university students. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements to our features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.
